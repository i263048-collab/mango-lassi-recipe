Mango Lassi Recipe

Description

Mango lassi is a cool, refreshing drink made with mangoes, yogurt, milk, and sugar. Blend the ingredients together to make a smooth and delicious drink.

Ingredients

  • 1.5 cups chopped mangos
  • 0.75 cup yogurt
  • 0.75 cup milk
  • 2–3 tablespoons sugar

Preparation Steps

Step Instructions
1Chop the mangoes.
2Add the mangoes, sugar, and milk to a blender.
3Blend until smooth.
4Add the yogurt.
5Blend again.
6Serve.
