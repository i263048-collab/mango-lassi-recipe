Popular Mango Varieties

Mangoes come in many varieties. Their size, colour, sweetness, and texture can differ.

🥭

Chaunsa

A well-known Pakistani mango, often enjoyed for its sweet, juicy flesh and aroma.

🥭

Sindhri

A large, golden-yellow mango variety grown in Sindh, usually available early in the season.

🥭

Anwar Ratol

A smaller mango known for its rich sweetness and distinctive fragrance.

🥭

Langra

A popular South Asian variety with green skin that can remain green when ripe.

Nutritional Benefits

Mangoes are a tasty fruit that can be part of a balanced diet. They provide several useful nutrients:

  • Vitamin C: Supports normal immune system function and helps the body make collagen.
  • Vitamin A: Important for normal vision and immune function.
  • Fibre: Helps support healthy digestion.
  • Water: Mangoes contain water, which contributes to daily fluid intake.

How to Choose a Ripe Mango

  1. Give it a gentle squeeze. A ripe mango often gives slightly when pressed gently.
  2. Smell near the stem. A ripe mango may have a sweet, fruity aroma.
  3. Check for damage. Avoid fruit with large bruises, leaking areas, or deep cuts.
  4. Do not judge by colour alone. Some varieties stay green even when ripe.

How to Store Mangoes

Not yet ripe

Keep whole mangoes at room temperature until they soften and develop a fruity aroma.

Ripe mangoes

Place ripe whole mangoes in the refrigerator to slow further ripening. Use them within a few days.

Cut mangoes

Store cut mango pieces in a covered container in the refrigerator and eat them promptly.
