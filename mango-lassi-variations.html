1. Classic Mango Lassi

The traditional creamy blend of mango, yogurt, and milk.

Ingredients (2 servings)

  • 1.5 cups chopped ripe mango
  • 1 cup plain yogurt
  • 0.5 cup milk
  • 1–2 tablespoons sugar, to taste
  • A few ice cubes (optional)

Method

  1. Add mango, yogurt, milk, and sugar to a blender.
  2. Blend until smooth and creamy.
  3. Add ice if desired, blend briefly, and serve chilled.
🌱

2. Dairy-Free Mango Lassi

A plant-based version made without dairy products.

Ingredients (2 servings)

  • 1.5 cups chopped ripe mango
  • 1 cup unsweetened coconut or soy yogurt
  • 0.5 cup oat, soy, or coconut drink
  • 1–2 teaspoons maple syrup (optional)
  • A pinch of cardamom (optional)

Method

  1. Put the mango, plant-based yogurt, and plant drink in a blender.
  2. Add maple syrup and cardamom if using.
  3. Blend until smooth. Taste, adjust, and serve cold.
🌼

3. Saffron & Cardamom Lassi

A fragrant version with warm spices and a little saffron.

Ingredients (2 servings)

  • 1.5 cups chopped ripe mango
  • 1 cup plain yogurt
  • 0.5 cup milk
  • 1–2 tablespoons sugar, to taste
  • A small pinch of saffron
  • 0.25 teaspoon ground cardamom

Method

  1. Soak saffron in one tablespoon of warm milk for a few minutes.
  2. Blend mango, yogurt, remaining milk, and sugar.
  3. Mix in the saffron milk and cardamom, then serve chilled.
🌹

4. Rose Mango Lassi

A floral and fruity drink with a gentle rose flavour.

Ingredients (2 servings)

  • 1.5 cups chopped ripe mango
  • 1 cup plain yogurt
  • 0.5 cup cold milk
  • 1 tablespoon rose syrup, or to taste
  • Ice cubes (optional)

Method

  1. Blend mango, yogurt, and milk until smooth.
  2. Add rose syrup and blend briefly.
  3. Taste before adding more syrup, then serve cold.
🍯

5. Naturally Sweetened Lassi

Lets ripe mango provide most of the sweetness, with no added sugar.

Ingredients (2 servings)

  • 2 cups very ripe chopped mango
  • 1 cup plain unsweetened yogurt
  • 0.5 cup cold milk or water
  • A pinch of cardamom (optional)

Method

  1. Add mango, yogurt, and milk or water to a blender.
  2. Add cardamom if desired.
  3. Blend until creamy. Taste and serve without adding sugar.
🧊

6. Frozen Mango Lassi

An extra-thick, frosty version that is almost like a smoothie.

Ingredients (2 servings)

  • 1.5 cups frozen mango chunks
  • 1 cup plain yogurt
  • 0.25–0.5 cup cold milk
  • 1 tablespoon honey or sugar (optional)

Method

  1. Let frozen mango sit for a few minutes so it is easier to blend.
  2. Blend mango, yogurt, and a little milk until thick and smooth.
  3. Add more milk if needed. Sweeten to taste and serve immediately.
